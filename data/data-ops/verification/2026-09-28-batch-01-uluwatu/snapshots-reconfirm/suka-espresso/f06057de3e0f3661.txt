Suka in Uluwatu is a lively, Australian-inspired café celebrated for its specialty coffee, wholesome menu, and laid-back atmosphere. Since opening in 2016, it has become a go-to spot for locals and visitors alike, offering a perfect mix of comfort and creativity. With ethically sourced coffee from across Indonesia, Suka serves expertly crafted brews alongside a menu that highlights fresh, seasonal ingredients.
Its welcoming design combines modern touches with rustic charm, creating a relaxed space that feels like home. Whether you're stopping by for a quick coffee or a leisurely meal, Suka captures the essence of ULUWATU's vibrant café culture.
