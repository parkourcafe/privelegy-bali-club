NATURAL WINE
Much like our approach to food, our natural wine selection is a reflection of Alchemy's holistic values and are a beautiful example of winemakers living in harmony with the land and animal kingdom. The wines we've carefully selected are not only vegan, but also free from added harmful chemicals and produced with organic practices. This means that each wine truly is “alive” and is in its purest expression possible; reflecting the personality of its soil, winemaker and local ecosystem. Enjoy a glass or two by themselves or paired with one of our delicious dishes.
