Dress Code and Kids Policy at Tropical Temptation
ARE KIDS ALLOWED?
To provide an exclusive atmosphere, our venue is reserved for adults only, ensuring a sophisticated experience tailored just for adults.
ARE THERE ANY DRESS CODE GUIDELINES AT TROPICAL TEMPTATION BEACH CLUB?
We encourage our guest to dress in their favorite RESORT WEAR and SMART CASUAL is the bare minimum. Certain items are not allowed, these include:
Football and sports shirts
Drink-branded shirt
Flip flops We reserve the right to deny entry to anyone who we believe is dressed inappropriately.
Appropriate swimwear is required for pool use, bikinis are welcomed, diving suits are not allowed.
IS THERE AN ENTRANCE FEE?
There is no entrance fee to enjoy our venue. However, a minimum spend is required for selected daybeds.
WHAT ARE THE HOURS OF OPERATION AT TROPICAL TEMPTATION BEACH CLUB?
We are open daily MONDAY – SUNDAY 7 AM – 9 PM
IS RESERVATION REQUIRED?
Yes, it will better to make advanced reservation for having the best table as expected
LOCKERS OR STORAGE FACILITIES?
Yes we do have locker or storage facilities on site
IS THERE A SECURITY SCREENING?
To help ensure good times for all, we check every bag coming into Tropical Temptation Beach Club. Our security team is there to create a safe environment and your cooperation is very much appreciated.
CAN I TAKE PROFESSIONAL PHOTOS? AND WHAT ABOUT A DRONE?
Drones and professional cameras (those including telephoto or zoom lenses) are permitted
ANY PROHIBITED ITEMS?
Illegal drugs, sharp items/ objects, explosive item, and outside food and beverages
IS THERE SHISHA?
There is a great selection of shisha. Enjoy!
ARE PETS ALLOWED AT TROPICAL TEMPTATION BEACH CLUB?
No pets are not allowed. As much as we love animals, we want to maintain sanitary and hygiene standards.
IS THERE ACCESS TO THE INTERNET?
Tropical Temptation Beach Club has a free wi-fi connection, and guests will be able to use it with password given
WHAT ARE THE ACCEPTED PAYMENT METHODS?
We accept cash, BCA Qris, all majority Debit Card and Credit Card
Payments made by wire transfer are accepted only upon approval and based on scheduled terms.
Payments by cheque are not accepted.

Only in the ground section

To secure your preferred date, please contact us as soon as you have approximate dates.
