FAQs
Q: Where is White Rock Beach Club?
Melasti Beach, Ungasan Uluwatu Bali – Indonesia, White Rock is located at the end of a dramatic cliff at Melasti Beach .
Q: What time does White Rock Beach Club open?
10:00 AM, to honor the sacred traditions of Nyepi, White Rock Beach Club will be closed one the day before Nyepi and Nyepi Day. During this time, the island of Bali will be in total silence with no lights or activity.
Q: What time does White Rock Beach Club close?
10:00 PM. Should you wish to extend your celebration|Party beyond standard hours for a private event or after-party, please contact a member of our sales team for further information and tailored arrangements.
Q: Is there any entrance fee?
No entry fee to the beach club except special event and New Year Eve Please note there is a small separate entrance fee to enter the Melasti Beach area (usually around IDR 10,000 for local – 15,000 for tourist ) collected by the local village at the main gate and there is spacious parking lot inside.
Q: What is the minimum spend and how does it work?
The minimum spend is a credit toward your food and beverage during your visit, it is including amenities fee (welcome drink, towel and access to guest facility) deducted from minimum spend at IDR 100K++ per person, It varies depending on the seating area (e.g., daybed Lagoon pool, daybed rock pool, sofa, restaurant tables and Bar or Indoor Party Suites: The ultimate private sanctuary with a breathtaking view of the ocean). Any amount spent above the minimum is simply added to your final bill. Especially Restaurant and Bar area, the minimum spend is IDR 500.000 for a certain period only
Q: Do I need to book in advance?
We highly recommend to book 48 hours prior to get best spot, best view and get 50% off minimum spend arrive before 2 pm only for selected area especially weekend is high demand and peak.
Q: Where should I book the daybed and table?
We highly recommend to book through our official website www.whiterockbali.com
Q: Is White Rock kids & Family friendly?
Yes! White Rock is a family-friendly destination. We offer a dedicated kids’ pool and a curated kids’ menu for our younger guests to enjoy. For safety, we kindly request that children be supervised by a parent or guardian at all times while using our facilities.
Q: What is daily event and activation at White Rock Beach Club?
Daily: Resident DJs from 12:30 PM.
Saturday & Sunday: Foam Party starting at 3:15 PM.
Special Days & Months: Dancers performance, Saxophone and Percussion, and Magician.
Q: Are pets allowed?
No Pets are Allowed, any kind of pets are not allowed inside the venue due to safety reasons and for the convenience of our guests.
Q: Is there any specific dress code at White Rock Beach Club?
Beach & Pool attire: Appropriate swimsuits and beachwear are required for all pools, wet areas, and the beach club premises. Underwear is strictly prohibited.
The restaurant requires appropriate attire: as it is designated for dining purposes, We maintain a consistently clean and dry dining environment. Swimsuits, towels, and wet clothing are not permitted inside the restaurant. For your convenience, changing rooms and locker rental are available at the beach club; guests are kindly requested to use these facilities before entering the restaurant, if necessary.
Q: During the rainy season, what is the venue option?
Private Party Suites (Highly Recommended), They are fully enclosed, air-conditioned indoor rooms that overlook the pool and ocean. They include a plush sofa, dining table, bathroom/shower, and a private balcony, capacity 10 – 25 people.
The main Restaurant area: The restaurant at White Rock is a large, covered structure. If you dine, you are usually allocated a table here, capacity 120 people. It is completely sheltered from the rain but remains open-air enough to feel the sea breeze.
The Ballroom / Multifunction Hall For very large groups or during massive storms, the club has indoor banquet spaces (like the Melasti rooms) that are fully indoor and Climate-controlled.
