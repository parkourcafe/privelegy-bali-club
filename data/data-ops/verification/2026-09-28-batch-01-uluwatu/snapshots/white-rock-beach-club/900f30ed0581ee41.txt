THE BIGGEST BEACH CLUB IN SOUTH OF BALI
Spectacular – absolute beachfront club located at the southernmost tip of Bali Island at Melasti Beach, Ungasan. Sprawled over 7,500 m2 below majestic cliffs of Melasti at one of the most beautiful sunset beaches in Bali, White Rock is an awe-inspiring club of the South. Accented with glittering infinity pools, excellent foods & drink menus, tasteful music and vibrant atmosphere from day into the late night.
