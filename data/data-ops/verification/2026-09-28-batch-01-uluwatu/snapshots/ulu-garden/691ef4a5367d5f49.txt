FAQ
Where is Ulu Garden located?
Ulu Garden is located on Padang Padang Beach Street, Uluwatu.
Do you accept walk-ins?
Yes, walk-ins are welcome. However, we recommend making a reservation in advance to secure your table, especially during peak hours.
Do you host regular events or special programs?
Yes. Each month, we curate special events inspired by the Balinese calendar. We also host signature programs such as Kampung Rasa a Sunday market featuring traditional Indonesian set menus and artisanal vendors and Fire & Wood, a live grilling experience with an Indonesian buffet selection.
Is parking available?
Yes. We provide a spacious parking area at the back of the venue, accommodating motorbikes, cars, and buses.
Do you accept cashless payments?
Yes. We accept card payments as well as cash for your convenience.
Do you host private events or celebrations?
Yes. Ulu Garden hosts birthdays, intimate gatherings, brand events, and community celebrations, supported by curated menus and cultural elements.
What kind of food do you serve?
We serve a modern Indonesian dining experience with a contemporary twist, using high-quality ingredients.
Do you have live music performances?
Yes. Live music performances are available daily.
What are your opening hours?
We are open daily for breakfast, lunch, and dinner, from 7 AM until late.
Are dogs allowed?
Yes. Ulu Garden is dog-friendly. Dogs are welcome with a leash.
Do you have a kids’ area?
Yes. We provide a dedicated kids’ corner for our younger guests.
Do you offer cultural performances?
Yes. We feature curated cultural performances from various regions across Indonesia.
How can I make a reservation?
Reservations can be made through our booking form or via the link in our Instagram bio.
Does Ulu Garden have social media?
Yes. You can find us on Instagram at @ulu.garden.
Do you offer vegan and gluten-free options?
Yes. Vegan and gluten-free options are available on our menu.
Is Ulu Garden family-friendly?
Yes. Our space is designed to be family friendly, with a kids’ corner and thoughtfully prepared dishes using quality ingredients.
Are you open to collaborations?
Yes. We welcome collaborations. Please reach out to our marketing team via email for further details.
