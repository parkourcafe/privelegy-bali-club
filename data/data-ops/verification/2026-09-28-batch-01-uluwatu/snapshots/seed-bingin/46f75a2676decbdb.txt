Menu Nusantara
Nusantara Menu is a journey through the islands of Indonesia, inviting you to discover the true flavors of this fascinating country. We highlight ginger flowers, Sulawesi coffee, the deep and powerful flavors of rendang, the night smoked sausage of Babi Guling, garden aromas, fresh spice blends, coconut oil, the acidity of tamarind, and many other flavorful treasures... Each spice is used like a perfume, inviting you to explore our culinary world.

Prices and dietary information
Reading the prices
All prices are in Indonesian rupiah (IDR), where k means thousand: 95k is IDR 95,000. Prices are subject to 10% government tax and 7% service charge, which is what the ++ on the set menu and cooking class prices refers to. Please inform our team of any allergies or dietary restrictions.
Vegetarian at Seed
Vegetarian dishes run through every menu rather than sitting in a separate section. From the dinner menu: Black Garlic Eggplant (90k), Curry Sayur Asem with tofu, pak choi, cauliflower and bamboo shoots (130k), Sumatran Sayur Rendang with tofu and tempe (145k), Sayuran (55k) and Empes Lombok, tofu and tempe sambal in a banana leaf (50k). Gyozas and bao buns come in vegetable fillings, there is a Vegetarian Burger with a mushroom patty (100k), and the breakfast menu is largely egg based. Tell the team what you avoid and the kitchen will work around it.
