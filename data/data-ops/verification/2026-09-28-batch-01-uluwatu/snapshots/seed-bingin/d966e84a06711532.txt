Description
Welcome to Seed, the place where you can experience food in a new way. Where their Head Chef Simon, brings his 13 years of experience and culinary craft from running a successful restaurant in French Alps and working as a luxury personal chef - to your table. What can you find on your plate? Using fresh produces from Seed’s garden, aromatics and spices, and local meat & seafood, Simon together with his team of very skilled cooks marries French cuisine with South East Asian flavors bringing unique and delicious dishes to you. Today - they invite you to start the journey towards a more flavourful life. Ask their lovely staff for the specialty of the day, and simply enjoy!
