The House
An intimate table where Asia meets France.
A short walk from Bingin Beach, Seed is a quiet dialogue between French technique and Southeast Asian soul. Chef Simon brings twenty years of global kitchens, including his own restaurant in the French Alps, to a stove shaped by the Bali garden behind it.
Pastry, ice cream, ferments and sauces are all made in house. Every dish begins in the garden; most end at a long wooden table under the string lights.

The Cuisine
Slow cooked, garden first.
From the morning omelette to the evening tasting, our kitchen begins in the Seed Garden. Balinese herbs, French technique, seafood landed at Jimbaran, and meats finished over bamboo smoke.
A pastry counter that rolls croissants at dawn. A bar that shakes Arak punches by twilight. One kitchen, one team, countless details.

How to arrive
Seed sits on Jalan Pantai Bingin, the road down to Bingin Beach, on Bali's Bukit peninsula in Uluwatu. It is a short walk up from the beach itself. Scooters and cars park on the road by the restaurant. Open in Google Maps .
When we are open
Every day, 7am to 11pm. Breakfast from 7am, happy hour 4pm to 7pm, dinner service from 4pm. The kitchen runs straight through, so there is no afternoon closure.
Good to know
Questions, answered.
Where is Seed restaurant located?
+ Seed is on Jalan Pantai Bingin in Pecatu, Uluwatu, on Bali's Bukit peninsula, a short walk up from Bingin Beach.
What are Seed's opening hours?
+ Seed is open daily from 7am to 11pm, serving breakfast, lunch, cocktails and dinner.
What kind of food does Seed serve?
+ Farm to table French Asian cooking: garden-grown herbs, wood-fired dishes and Indonesian-inspired plates, with a bar of house-infused spirits and cocktails.
How do I book a table at Seed?
+ You can reserve a table online through Seed's SevenRooms booking page, linked from every "Book a table" button on seedbingin.com.
Does Seed host weddings and private events?
+ Yes. Seed hosts weddings under the old tree, retreats, full restaurant privatisations and corporate or brand dinners. Send an inquiry through the form on our Events and Private Dining page and the team responds within 24 hours.
Does Seed offer cooking classes?
+ Yes, Seed runs hands-on cooking classes, bookable via WhatsApp.
