OUR WAY
At Alchemy, our mission is to transform people, the planet, and the community around us by starting with ourselves. We believe that by caring for our health and well-being, we can create a better world for others. Through our delicious plant-based food, traditional yoga practices, and signature health services, we provide access, inspiration, and guidance. Our offerings are designed to cleanse, heal, and revitalize, allowing individuals to reconnect with themselves and experience the vibrant energy of being alive. Our commitment to sourcing local and ethical ingredients allows us to be the change and contribute to making the world a little bit better with every visit to Alchemy.

OUR FLAVOURS
At Alchemy, you can enjoy fresh and nourishing food full of nutrients and life force. Our suppliers have been carefully selected for their organic and regenerative practices and our takeaway containers are all biodegradable. We believe that by taking care of Mother Earth she takes care of us. A lot of dishes on our menu are raw, which means that they are not cooked or heated above 43 degrees Celsius/110 Fahrenheit. We also have some gently cooked food options for those who like. Our menu is completely plant based and gluten free.

Alchemy Wellness & Rejuvenatio
ALCHEMY WELLNESS & REJUVENATION
Alchemy Wellness and Rejuvenation is a holistic wellness centre devoted to supporting the body, mind and emotional system as an interconnected whole.
Its philosophy is grounded in the understanding that lasting wellbeing does not come from treating symptoms in isolation, but from addressing physical health and emotional balance together, with care and precision. Our wellness centre offers a wide range of therapies, from colon hydrotherapy and IV nutrient support to integrative consultations and somatic-based treatments, all designed to gently restore vitality and resilience.
Alchemy is for those who feel depleted, overwhelmed, or out of alignment, as well as for people who simply want to maintain their health in a more conscious and preventative way. With two welcoming premises, one in Canggu and one in Ubud, guests are able to choose the setting that best supports their healing journey, whether they are seeking deep restoration, recovery, or a return to balance in everyday life.
OUR SERVICES
​ Colon hydrotherapy
Experience gentle, effective colon hydrotherapy at Alchemy Wellness Bali. This safe treatment helps remove built up waste, easing bloating, supporting digestion, and restoring energy. Many clients notice clearer skin and greater vitality. By supporting detoxification and nutrient absorption, colon hydrotherapy provides a calm, restorative reset for gut health and wellbeing.
​
Infrared sauna & Ice bath ​
Available in our private Recovery Area, we offer infrared sauna and ice bath therapy at Alchemy Wellness Canggu Alchemy. Together they support detoxification, circulation, lower inflammation, muscle recovery, relaxation and enhanced stress resilience
.
Balinese massage
Balinese massage is a deeply relaxing, full body treatment combining gentle stretches, acupressure, and flowing strokes. It eases muscle tension, improves circulation, and calms the nervous system. Ideal for restoring balance and easing fatigue, this traditional massage leaves the body grounded, relaxed, and quietly reenergised after everyday physical stress.
​
Holistic Therapies
Alongside physical therapies, Alchemy Wellness also offers integrated mind and body treatments that support deep healing and regulation. These include naturopathy, somatic healing, acupuncture, osteopathy, family constellations and breathwork therapy. Together, these approaches work with the nervous system, emotional patterns, and the body&rsquo;s innate capacity to restore balance, supporting long term wellbeing beyond symptom relief.

OUR PROJECTS
Alchemy Academy is a raw vegan culinary school nestled in the vibrant jungle town of Ubud, on the tropical island of Bali, Indonesia. We offer a variety of courses and retreats on the art of plant-based cuisine, Including chef certifications levels 1 and 2, healing detox retreats, raw chocolate classes, vegan cheese workshops, gluten free baking courses, pop up dining events, yoga, meditation and more.
