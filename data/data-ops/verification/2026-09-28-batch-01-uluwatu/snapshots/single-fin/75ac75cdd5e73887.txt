ULUWATU SINGLE FIN CLASSIC 17-18 August 2024
As one of Bali’s most prestigious events, this year’s Uluwatu Single Fin Classic promises to be the best yet. World champions Mark Occhilupo, Joel Parkinson, Mikey February, Joel Tudor, and Tosh Tudor, along with iconic Indonesian surfers, will join the lineup.
The Single Fin Classic celebrates the history and iconic visuals that put Uluwatu on the surfing map in 1972. All boards must be original pre-1990s designs, reflecting the era when single fin surfboards were the only choice.
SFC kicks off on Friday, August 16, with a panel talk featuring Occy, Parko, Mike February, Joel, and Tosh Tudor. Daily live music will play from noon until late. With a favorable long-range forecast, this year’s Uluwatu Single Fin Classic promises an exciting weekend for all Bali lovers.
