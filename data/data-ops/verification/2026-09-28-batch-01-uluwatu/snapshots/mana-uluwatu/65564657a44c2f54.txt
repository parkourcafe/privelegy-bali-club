Restaurant
Mana Uluwatu
Mana Restaurant & Pool is perched high on the cliffs of Bali’s Bukit peninsula, providing spectacular ocean views and flavorful cuisine from the local land and sea. Mana Uluwatu proudly stands amidst the stunning grounds of Uluwatu Surf Villas and is open to outside guests. Dining is offered with a formal experience in the main upstairs dining area and also offers a more casual experience by the infinity pool, each with its own exquisite menu!
Mana invites you on a culinary voyage from coast to coast, featuring an array of sustainably sourced seafood, free-range poultry, and grass-fed beef, all masterfully infused with indigenous Indonesian spices. This culinary journey also extends its support to local farmers from Bali’s hinterland regions. All these mouthwatering dishes are presented in a captivating open-air pavilion meticulously crafted from reclaimed teak and ironwood.
Open from 7am-11pm daily serving breakfast, lunch & dinner with ocean views and sea breezes.
