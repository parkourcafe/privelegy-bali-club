Editorial Note
About This Restaurant
Laggas has moved from our old Seminyak location to Jl. Pantai Bingin Uluwatu, rising from the ashes for a second time. Laggas serves a wide range of dining options from dumplings to noodles, appetizers to salads and desserts to drinks. Our "Asian Fusion' concept allows us to play with a variety of Asian and Southeast Asian flavor profiles to create mouth watering dishes for your enjoyment. This concept has been a recipe for success for over 8 years from it's inception as Fat Gajah under the late Chef Agung Nugroho through it's current rebirth as Laggas with Chef Yudi. We hope to serve our past, present and future guests a delightful meal at our new casual, chill and airy location in Bingin.
