CONDITIONS OF ENTRY
​
NO CLIMBING, SITTING OR STANDING ON THE GLASS EDGE OF THE POOL
KEEP YOUR CHEST BELOW THE EDGE OF THE POOL AT ALL TIMES!
Please note that non-compliance with this rule will result in immediate removal from the premises without refund. We take your safety seriously.
We have zero tolerance for risk-taking behaviour and our security team will confiscate cameras and delete any photos of dangerous activity.
​
HOURS OF OPERATION
10:00 - 9.00 pm | last order 8.00 pm.
DRESS CODE
Beachside smart casual. Alcohol-branded attire is not permitted & appropriate swimwear is required for pool use.
SWIMWEAR: Swimsuits, Bikinis, and Burqinis are permitted. Clothing (non-swimwear) and nudity is not permitted.
​
EXTERNAL FOOD & BEVERAGE
No food and beverage from outside is permitted, with the exception of food & drinks for children under 3 years old.
PHOTOGRAPHY
For the privacy of our resort guests, please note that drone photography is not permitted in oneeighty°.
SMOKING
Smoking is permitted in outdoor areas, but please be mindful of the comfort of other guests at all times. Smoking is not permitted in the one-eighty restaurant.
CHILDREN
Children under 12 are not permitted on the VIP deck and must be closely supervised by their parents/guardians at all times. Children under 2 years of age are required to wear a swim diaper.
​
PETS
Pets are not permitted at one-eighty for the comfort of all of our guests.
ABOUT US
​Located within the estate of exclusive The edge villas and built around a stunning glass bottom lagoon set to redefine the concept of the infinity pool, oneeighty° is a celebration of sophisticated beach culture 162 m above the Indian Ocean.
An extensive VIP deck, VIP rooftop lounge, sand lounge and enclosed bar & restaurant, all overlooking the most breathtaking panorama imaginable.
POOL RULES
​
SWIMMING IS AT YOUR OWN RISK
For your safety, please be careful on the rock area at the lagoon at one time
For your safety, inflatable pool toys/floats are not permitted for use in oneeighty° Skypool
No running or diving into the pool
Children must be closely supervised by their parents or guardians at all times.
No glass is permitted in or around the pool
Appropriate swimwear is essential & we request that you please shower before pool use.
Please consider other guests and do not block the pool view for extended periods.
Discharge of fecal matter in the pool will result in a fine of USD 6,500. Babies are required to wear swim diapers
