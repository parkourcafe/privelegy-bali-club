Uluwatu is a land of dramatic cliffs and legendary sunsets, and perched atop one of its most breathtaking perches is the One Eighty Day Club Bali . Famed for its glass-bottom sky pool that juts out over the Indian Ocean, this venue has become an icon of Bali’s luxury scene. But is it worth the hype?
This comprehensive review dives into everything you need to know about the atmosphere, drinks, service, and overall value at the one eighty day club bali , exploring what makes this Uluwatu bar with an ocean view a must-visit destination.
Atmosphere & Vibe at One Eighty Day Club Bali
Located within The Edge Bali resort in Pecatu, the one eighty day club bali offers an experience defined by its staggering location. The club is perched 162 meters above the Indian Ocean, providing panoramic views of crashing waves and distant surfers. The neighborhood itself is more about natural drama than bustling urban nightlife; at night, the venue transforms into a serene, elevated oasis, far removed from the traffic of Seminyak.
The design is a masterclass in sophisticated beach culture. The centerpiece is the jaw-dropping, glass-bottomed sky pool that extends six meters over the cliff edge, cascading from a series of ponds. Seating is strategically placed to maximize the view, with options ranging from sunbeds by the pool and a sand lounge to a multi-level VIP deck and a rooftop lounge with sofa-style seating. As the sun sets after 5 PM, uplit coconut palms cast a warm glow, and the atmosphere shifts from a vibrant day club to a tranquil cocktail lounge.

Drinks & Menu
While a current menu is best confirmed on-site, the bar at one eighty day club bali has built a reputation for high-quality beverages. It is home to what they describe as an “all-star mixologist team” crafting stellar cocktails. Reviews frequently praise the drinks, with guests specifically enjoying a standout coco mojito. An older menu snippet points to creative options like the “Infinity” cocktail, made with prosecco and strawberry liqueur.
Beyond cocktails, this luxury day club boasts a Wine Spectator Award-winning wine list, curated by Nyoman Suwinda Yasa, named Bali’s Best Sommelier in 2016. Pricing aligns with a premium venue, with older menus showing cocktails around 110K IDR and glasses of wine starting at 120K IDR. The experience is less about a vast menu and more about enjoying a perfectly executed drink in an unparalleled setting.
Service & Bartender Expertise
Service at the one eighty day club Bali is generally friendly, attentive, and professional, reflecting its luxury positioning. Guests often note that the staff is polite and gracious, contributing to a seamless experience from arrival to departure. The expertise of the bar team is evident, with many visitors praising the quality and consistency of the cocktails, even during busy periods.
Reservations are strongly recommended, as the venue operates with an entrance fee that functions as a food and beverage credit. General admission typically comes with a four-hour time limit, encouraging guests to book VIP packages for a full day of relaxation. While most find the service quick and helpful, some have noted that staff can be keen to upsell on arrival and that the addition of service charges and taxes (“++”) isn’t always clearly communicated upfront.

Events & Crowd Patterns
One Eighty Day Club Bali is best described as a lounge-style dayclub with a focus on relaxation, not a high-energy party spot. The soundtrack consists of chilled background music, creating a sophisticated ambiance that allows for conversation. The venue explicitly states it does not have daily live entertainment, instead focusing on the natural beauty of its surroundings, making it one of the best sunset spots in Uluwatu.
The crowd is a mix of tourists, couples, and small groups seeking a luxurious day out. Weekdays are generally busy but with plenty of space, while weekends and sunset hours attract a larger crowd. During festive seasons like Christmas and New Year’s, the club hosts special events with a more vibrant, celebratory energy. The dress code is beachside smart casual, though clothing with alcohol branding is not permitted.
Location & Value
You can find the one eighty day club bali at Jl. Pura Goa Lempeh in Pecatu, Uluwatu , within the grounds of The Edge resort. The best way to get there is by car, private driver, or ride-hailing service, as public transport is limited in this remote part of Bali. On-site parking is available for guests.
The club is open daily from 10 AM to 9 PM, with last orders taken at 8 PM. The best times to visit are weekdays for a more relaxed vibe or just before sunset for the spectacular golden-hour views. While it is an expensive destination by Bali standards, most guests feel the experience offers good value. The entry fee is almost entirely redeemable for food and drinks, and the unforgettable views from the iconic sky pool make it a worthwhile indulgence.
What Customers Are Saying
Based on a high volume of overwhelmingly positive reviews for one eighty day club bali , guests describe the experience as “amazing,” “breathtaking,” and an “unforgettable must-visit.” The vast majority of visitors leave 4 or 5-star ratings and frequently express a desire to return.
Recurring Praise
Spectacular Views: The panoramic, cliffside ocean view is the single most praised feature and is consistently described as the highlight of the visit.
Exceptional Service: The staff receives universal commendation for being friendly, attentive, professional, and welcoming. A lifeguard named Jack is mentioned by name in numerous reviews as a standout employee who goes above and beyond for guests.
High-Quality Food & Drinks: Guests frequently describe the food as “delicious” and “exquisite,” with generous portions. Popular menu items include burger sliders, ribs, pizza, and ceviche.
Relaxing & Luxurious Vibe: The atmosphere is noted for being tranquil, chill, and upscale rather than a loud party scene. The background music is often complimented for being at a pleasant volume that allows for conversation.
Fair Value: The minimum spend is generally considered “worth it” for the high-quality experience, stunning location, and amenities like towels and comfortable daybeds.
Cleanliness: Visitors consistently mention that the venue is clean and well-maintained.
Common Complaints
Poor Pool Condition: The most significant complaint is that the signature glass-bottom pool’s panels are often dirty, blurry, or not transparent , which diminishes the unique experience. Several reviews also mention a strong smell of chlorine.
4-Hour Time Limit: A frequent point of frustration is the 4-hour time restriction on general admission, which makes many guests feel rushed and unable to fully relax.
Signs of Wear and Tear: A few reviews note that some furniture or towels appear worn or have stains, detracting from the otherwise premium feel.
Strict Rules & Zoned Access: Some guests felt the welcome was unwelcoming due to an immediate focus on rules and restricted access to “VIP only” areas.
Crowded for Photoshoots: While recognized as an “Instagrammable” destination, the pool area can become crowded with people focused on taking photos, making it less relaxing for others.
A Hidden Gem Nearby: Coco Lifestyle Resort
After a day spent soaking in the dramatic views at Oneeighty, the relaxed yet sophisticated energy of Uluwatu invites you to linger. Just minutes away in Bingin, Coco Lifestyle Resort captures this same spirit, offering a perfect blend of modern living and tropical style. It’s an ideal home base for those looking to extend their experience beyond a single afternoon.
This stylish resort features 62 fully managed apartments designed for comfort, community, and wellness. Imagine unwinding after your day club visit with a swim in the Rooftop Infinity Pool , catching another incredible sunset from a different vantage point. For those who need to balance work and play, the modern Co-Working Space provides a productive sanctuary.
The focus on holistic living continues at the resort’s wellness area, where you can recharge in the fully equipped Gym, Sauna, Ice Bath & Massage Room . Later, you can gather with friends for a nourishing meal at the Backyard Restaurant & Lounge , an open-air space that embodies Bali’s communal charm. It’s a place designed not just to stay, but to live the Uluwatu lifestyle to its fullest.
Final Thoughts
Oneeighty is a premier destination for anyone seeking breathtaking views, high-quality cocktails, and an atmosphere of relaxed luxury. It’s particularly well-suited for couples on a romantic getaway, small groups celebrating a special occasion, or solo travelers looking to indulge. The glass-bottom pool is an architectural marvel that truly has to be seen to be believed.
While a visit is an unforgettable experience on its own, pairing your day at the one eighty day club bali with a stay at a complementary location like Coco Lifestyle Resort can elevate your entire trip. It allows you to fully immerse yourself in the unique coastal energy that makes this part of Bali one of the most sought-after destinations in the world.
FAQ
Where is the one eighty day club bali located?
Oneeighty is perched on a clifftop 162 meters above the Indian Ocean in Uluwatu. You can find it at Jl. Pura Goa Lempeh in Pecatu, located within the luxury grounds of The Edge Bali resort.
What is the atmosphere and vibe like?
It's a sophisticated and tranquil lounge-style dayclub focused on relaxation, not a high-energy party spot. The vibe is defined by its stunning panoramic views and chilled background music that allows for conversation. After 5 PM, it transforms into a serene cocktail lounge.
What is the main attraction at the one eighty day club bali?
The centerpiece and most famous feature is the glass-bottom sky pool, which extends six meters over the cliff edge, offering an unparalleled view of the ocean directly below.
What kind of drinks and food are available?
The bar has an "all-star mixologist team" crafting high-quality cocktails, with the coco mojito being a guest favorite. There is also a Wine Spectator Award-winning wine list. Guest reviews praise the food as "delicious" with generous portions, mentioning items like burger sliders, ribs, pizza, and ceviche.
How does pricing and entry work at the one eighty day club bali?
Oneeighty operates with an entrance fee that serves as a food and beverage credit. While prices are at a premium level, most guests feel the minimum spend is "worth it" for the experience. Note that service charges and taxes ("++") are added to the final bill.
What are the opening hours and the best time to visit?
The club is open daily from 10 AM to 9 PM, with last orders taken at 8 PM. Visit on a weekday for a more relaxed atmosphere, or arrive just before sunset for the spectacular golden-hour views.
Do I need to make a reservation?
Yes, reservations are strongly recommended. Standard general admission includes a 4-hour time limit, which some guests find rushing. If you plan to stay longer, consider booking a VIP package for a full day of access.
What is the service like?
Guest reviews consistently describe the service as exceptional. The staff is noted for being friendly, attentive, professional, and welcoming. A lifeguard named Jack is frequently mentioned by name in reviews as a standout employee who goes above and beyond for guests.
Are there any common complaints I should be aware of?
While overwhelmingly positive, some recurring complaints include:
The glass panels of the signature pool can sometimes be dirty or blurry, diminishing the view.
The 4-hour time limit on general admission can feel rushed.
Some furniture and towels may show signs of wear and tear.
The pool area can get crowded with people focused on taking photos.
Is there a dress code?
Yes, the dress code is beachside smart casual. Importantly, clothing with alcohol branding is not permitted in the venue.
How do I get to the one eighty day club bali?
Since it's in a more remote part of Uluwatu with limited public transport, the best way to get there is by car, private driver, or a ride-hailing app. On-site parking is available for guests.
Is there a recommended place to stay nearby to complement the visit?
Yes, to extend the sophisticated Uluwatu experience, Coco Lifestyle Resort in nearby Bingin is an ideal choice. It offers stylish apartments and amenities designed to blend nightlife with relaxation and wellness, such as a Rooftop Infinity Pool and a spa area.
How does staying at Coco Lifestyle Resort enhance a trip to the one eighty day club bali?
After an afternoon at the day club, you can retreat to Coco Lifestyle Resort to continue enjoying the Uluwatu vibe. You can watch another sunset from the infinity pool or unwind at the wellness sanctuary, which features a gym, sauna, ice bath, and massage room, turning your bar visit into a complete luxury lifestyle escape.
