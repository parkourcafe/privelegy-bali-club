BGS Uluwatu
Opened in late 2019, BGS Uluwatu sits just minutes from Uluwatu Beach, a go-to spot for a strong cup before or after your surf session around the Bukit area. Come early, stay awhile, and soak in the rhythm of Bali’s southern surf coast.

Our Story
BGS Uluwatu sits near the cliffs and iconic waves of the Bukit area, a destination that draws surfers from all over the world. It was made for those chasing swells and those looking to slow down.
Whether you stop by before paddling out or after your last set, this is where strong coffee and calm energy come together. From pro surfers to weekend explorers, everyone finds a reason to return.
This little cliffside corner has become a favorite for locals, travelers, and the wider surf community across the Bukit. Whether you’re here for the waves or just passing through, BGS Uluwatu is where stories start, friendships grow, and good coffee fuels it all.
You’ll get what you need in one place. Surf gear, curated merch, cold drinks, and a team that always makes you feel welcome. BGS Uluwatu isn’t just close to the ocean, it’s part of the surf itself.
