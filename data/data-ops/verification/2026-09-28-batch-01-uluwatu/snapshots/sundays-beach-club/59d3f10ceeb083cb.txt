01 What Is Sundays Beach Club Like?
Ungasan｜Beach club
Sundays Beach Club is a white-sand beach club set below the cliffs of southern Uluwatu in Ungasan. It is less about sitting beside a pool and more about building a full beach day around the ocean, sand, inclinator ride, beanbags, watersports and sunset bonfire.
It suits travelers who want to stay close to the sea, enjoy the cliff-and-beach scenery that makes Uluwatu feel special, and spend a long stretch from daytime into sunset. If you want a loud pool party, this is not the obvious first pick; if you want a resort-style beach day without staying overnight, it is easy to choose.
02 Entrance and Overall Vibe
The Sundays experience starts as soon as you arrive at the clifftop. The beach sits below, and guests ride the inclinator down to the club. That short descent is part of the experience: the cliff, ocean color and white sand gradually come into view.
Once you reach the beach, the center of the venue is sand and sea. Beanbags and umbrellas keep it relaxed, while The Ungasan setting gives it a polished resort feel. It feels closer to using a seaside resort for the day than simply visiting a beach bar.

03 Pool, Beach and Daytime Flow
For general guests, Sundays is built around beach time rather than a pool scene. Swimming, snorkeling, kayaking, stand-up paddleboarding and resting on the sand are the natural flow of the day.
Watersports depend on tides and weather. If you want to spend real time in the water, arrive early. If you want to stay for the bonfire and evening music, plan a slower afternoon-to-night visit.

04 Seat Types and Minimum Spend
Sundays splits seat planning between walk-in Daily Beach Pass access and pre-booked VIP Experiences. The Daily Beach Pass is IDR 800K for adults with IDR 500K F&B credit, and IDR 400K for children aged 5-11 with IDR 250K F&B credit.
The seven VIP products are Wood Deck King/Queen, Sand Deck King/Queen, Cabana, Bungalow and Beach House. All provide all-day access, with product-specific capacity, age rules and F&B credit. Packages require full advance payment and are non-refundable, non-cancellable and non-changeable once confirmed.

05 Recommended Time to Visit
For pool time and daytime views, start from midday to early afternoon. For dining and the late-day atmosphere, late afternoon into evening works better. Confirm seat and pool-use terms when booking because they can vary by date. If sunset is the target, plan to arrive earlier and account for travel time.

06 Food and Drinks
Sundays works for more than light beach snacks. It is easy to stay from lunch into afternoon drinks, and the Daily Beach Pass includes F&B credit, so it makes more sense to think of the entry as part of a food-and-drink beach plan.
Arrive around lunch for a fuller meal, come later for lighter plates and cocktails, or stay until evening and pair drinks with the bonfire and music.
07 Photo Spots
The best photo moments are the clifftop arrival, the inclinator, white sand and beanbags, clear water and the sunset bonfire.
For bright beach photos, aim for morning through early afternoon. For moodier shots, sunset works well as the cliff shadow and bonfire change the whole atmosphere.

14 FAQ
Does Sundays Beach Club have a pool? For general guests, Sundays is more of a beach experience than a pool club. Think ocean, sand, watersports and bonfire.
How much is the Daily Beach Pass? Adults are IDR 800K and children aged 5-11 are IDR 400K. Adults receive IDR 500K F&B credit and children receive IDR 250K F&B credit.
Is Sundays good for families? Yes, but the sea is the main attraction, so supervision matters. Plan around tide, sun and the ride home.
Should I book in advance? For casual visits, walk-in entry works. For fixed seating, anniversaries, groups or VIP spaces, advance booking is better.
