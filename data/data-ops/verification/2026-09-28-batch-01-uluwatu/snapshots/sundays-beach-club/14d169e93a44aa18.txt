Activities
At Sundays, it’s in our nature to turn every day into something special. Beyond the beach and our renowned dining, you’ll find watersports, bonfires, live music and beachfront cinema — all woven together to create a relaxed rhythm that feels as natural as the tide.
WATERSPORTS
Explore the crystal clear lagoon and scattered rock pools along the reef. Kayaks, stand up paddle boards, snorkeling or swimming – everything you need is on us. Subject to tides and availability.
SUNSET BONFIRES
We fire up our beach bonfires daily at sunset. Grab a beanbag and toast some marshmallows. Is there a better way to end the day? Daily from 6:30 PM.
Inclinator
Your journey to the water’s edge at Sundays is as spectacular as the destination itself thanks to our custom inclinator lift.

DAILY ENTERTAINMENT

SUNDAYS WELLNESS

BAR AND DINING
Ocean's Bounty: A New Culinary Journey
Discover a menu that redefines beach club cuisine with bold, boundary-pushing dishes. From our ocean-view bar to your spot on the sand, savor the freshest flavors, with ingredients sourced directly from the ocean just steps away, celebrating the incredible bounty of our local waters and the dedicated fishermen who bring it to us. Complement your meal with a signature cocktail, craft beer, cider, or a carefully curated international wine.

The Ungasan Clifftop Resort
Luxury Villas
Set high above the ocean on Bali’s Bukit Peninsula , The Ungasan offers a private retreat defined by space, serenity and sweeping sea views. Each villa is designed for quiet indulgence, with its own pool, open-air living and attentive service that anticipates every need. Below the cliffs, guests have exclusive access to the celebrated Sundays Beach Club, where white sand, calm waters and relaxed dining create the perfect coastal escape. Whether your day is spent drifting in the pool, exploring the shoreline or enjoying sunset by the fire, The Ungasan delivers a sense of escape that lingers long after you leave.
