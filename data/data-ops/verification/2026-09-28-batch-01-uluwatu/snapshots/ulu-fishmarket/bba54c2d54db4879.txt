From Hawaii to Bali
Ulu Fish Market it’s a combination of the Paia Fish Market (est. 1989) with a fusion Japanese and Peruvian sushi cuisine. With signature cocktails and a great atmosphere we are in the heart of Uluwatu. Our goal is simple: To continue to stay true to our roots of sourcing local ingredients and serving generous and delicious portions, making every bite a journey of the sens.
