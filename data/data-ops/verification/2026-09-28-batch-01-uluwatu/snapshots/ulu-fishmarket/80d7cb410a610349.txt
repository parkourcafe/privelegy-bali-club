Description
Every dish at Ulu Fish Market is a tribute to the ocean. Locally sourced ingredients are transformed into generous, delicious portions that tell the story of their coastal roots. Their restaurant is designed to be a vibrant gathering spot where surfers, tourists, and fish enthusiasts come together. A place where live music and friendly chatter evoke the carefree energy of the beach. Every visit feels like joining a family that celebrates the sea in all its splendor. Ulu Fishmarket is a tribute to the enduring bonds of community, culture, and ocean. Their mission is: to honor their roots, cherish the sea, and craft a warm atmosphere where music, delicious flavours, and people come together, leaving every guests feeling at ease, and truly at home.

House Policies Ulu Fish Market
1. Reservation
For booking required after 6:30pm , please reach us directly at +62 822-6646-9790
Daily Reservation time maximum at 7:30pm due high season
2. Outside Food & Beverages
Guests are not permitted to bring any outside food or drinks onto the premises. Only items offered by Ulu Fish Market may be consumed.
3. Dress Code
Shirts are required in the restaurant area during dinner service. Kindly respect our dress code to ensure a pleasant dining experience for all.
4. Reservation Policy
Your reserved table will be held for up to 15 minutes past the scheduled time. In case of late arrival or no-show, we reserve the right to release your table without guarantee of alternative seating.
5. Personal Belongings
Ulu Fish Market is not responsible for the loss, damage, or theft of any personal items on the premises.
6. Pet Policy
Pets are welcome but must remain on a leash at all times.
7. Smoking Policy
Smoking and vaping are strictly prohibited indoors.
8. Special Requests
We do our best to accommodate special requests; however, they are not guaranteed and are subject to availability and the discretion of our team.
9. Group Reservations
For parties larger than 10 guests, please contact us directly at +62 822-6646-9790 to arrange your group booking.
