CHILDREN POLICY
El Kabron Bali is an adult-oriented venue. Children are allowed at El Kabron Bali only in the Restaurant Area with the following regulations:
Children must be accompanied by a responsible adult at all times.
Children are allowed ONLY in the Restaurant Area, no exceptions can be made to this policy. Children are strictly not allowed in our adults-only Hedonism Lounge Area (Pool Area including dance floor) and adults-only Sunset Theater Area.
Please, kindly note that our pool is adults-only (for guests over 18 years old). Children are not allowed to use the swimming pool at all times. For the security purpose no exceptions can be made to this policy. Minimum spend applies to all guests including children (over 5 years old), nannies, and bodyguards.
