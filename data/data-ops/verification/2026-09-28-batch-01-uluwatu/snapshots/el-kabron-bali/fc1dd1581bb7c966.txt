VENUE POLICIES & FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
Reservation Policies Reservations will be held for up to 15 minutes past the booking time. Guests late by 15 minutes or more will be offered seats on arrival, subject to availability.
All reservations are non-refundable and non-transferrable to another date. In case of inclement weather, reservations in Hedonism Lounge and Sunset Theater will be transferred indoors inside the spacious oceanfront Restaurant Area based on availability.
For large groups and reservations more than 6 months in advance, please contact us at info@elkabron.com or via WhatsApp at +62-813-3723-5750.
Location El Kabron Bali is located at Jl. Pantai Cemongkak, Pecatu, Bali 80361.
Hours of Operation Every day from 11:00 until midnight.
Traveling to & from El Kabron Bali We strongly suggest to all our guests to arrange their own transportation to and from El Kabron Bali prior to the journey. Please note that operators such as Blue Bird, Uluwatu taxi, Grab and GO-JEK are allowed to do drop-offs ONLY. Local taxi service is the only available option to pick up passengers as per local regulations, we can only assist to arrange pick up from the venue through local taxi service. El Kabron Bali does not manage any transport services and therefore, cannot be held liable for any related issues.
El Kabron Bali Children Policy El Kabron Bali is an adult-oriented venue. Children are allowed at El Kabron Bali only in the Restaurant Area with the following regulations: Children must be accompanied by a responsible adult at all times. Children are allowed ONLY in the Restaurant Area, no exceptions can be made to this policy. Children are strictly not allowed in our adults-only Hedonism Lounge Area (Pool Area including dance floor) and adults-only Sunset Theater Area. Please, kindly note that our pool is adults-only (for guests over 18 years old). Children are not allowed to use the swimming pool at all times. For the security purpose no exceptions can be made to this policy. Minimum spend applies to all guests including children (over 5 years old), nannies, and bodyguards.
Special Dietary Requests Please inform us of any dietary restrictions or requirements prior to your visit and we will do our best to accommodate you.
Dress Code Hedonism Lounge (Pool Area): Beachwear, Resort Wear, Smart Casual, or Elegant Attire is welcome. Restaurant Area & Sunset Theater: Smart Casual, Resort Wear, or Elegant Attire is required. Swimwear is not permitted in these areas, and men must wear a shirt or cover-up. Please note: T-shirts or singlets with beverage logos, athletic wear, wedding dresses, uniforms, excessively revealing clothing, matching outfits with corporate or branded logos, and themed costumes are strictly not allowed in the venue. The venue reserves the right to deny entry for dress code violations not outlined in this policy.
Outside Food & Beverages Outside food and beverages, including cakes, are strictly not permitted to be brought in the venue. No exceptions will be made to this policy. Please note that a corkage fee is not available.
Outside Decorations Outside decorations, including but not limited to balloons, signs, and thematic decor elements, are strictly prohibited in the venue.
Forms Of Payment We accept cash (IDR) and the following credit & debit cards: VISA, MasterCard.
Wheelchair Access The Oceanfront Restaurant and restroom facilities are accessible to wheelchairs.
Filming & Photography Policy Smartphone cameras are welcome in El Kabron Bali. We do not permit hand-held cameras, selfie sticks, video cameras, drones and other professional or amateur filming equipment for privacy and security reasons. For the safety and comfort of all guests, any such equipment will be held at reception for the duration of your stay. No exceptions will be made to this policy. All types of photoshoots and commercial filming are strictly prohibited. The venue reserves the right to stop smartphone filming if it causes a disturbance to other guests, if filming looks like a photoshoot, or violates any other venue regulations.
