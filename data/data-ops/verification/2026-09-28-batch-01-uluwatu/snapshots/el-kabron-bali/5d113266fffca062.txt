Iconic Luxury Party Beach Club
with the best sunsets in the world
Uluwatu, Bali
El Kabron Bali is a Luxury Party Beach Club & Mediterranean Oceanfront Restaurant in Uluwatu famous for the best sunsets in the world and its exclusive, one-of-a-kind ambience.
Established in the heart of Uluwatu in 2011, El Kabron Bali offers a unique hedonistic paradise under the palms with phenomenal sunsets all year round, high-end oceanfront dining, and signature daily Sunset Parties with live performances.

HEDONISM LOUNGE
(POOL AREA)
El Kabron Bali Hedonism Lounge is an exclusive adult-only pool area featuring oceanfront daybeds and sofas by the infinity pool. Spend a day in paradise and enjoy iconic vibes with live saxophone and DJs.

FAMOUS SUNSET THEATER
Located at the edge of the ocean, El Kabron Sunset Theater is designed for a unique, theater-like sunset dining experience. Romantic dinners at Sunset Theater are known as one of the best things to do in Bali.

OCEANFRONT RESTAURANT
El Kabron Bali Mediterranean Oceanfront Restaurant is the most-loved place in Uluwatu for lunch and dinner. Fresh seafood, oysters on ice, steak and paella - all served with panoramic ocean views and sunsets.

SUNSET PARTIES IN ULUWATU
El Kabron Bali is home to the well-known White Sunset Party in Uluwatu and special daily events. Each party brings phenomenal vibes with all-day DJ sets, an iconic sunset show, and live performances with dancing by the ocean.
