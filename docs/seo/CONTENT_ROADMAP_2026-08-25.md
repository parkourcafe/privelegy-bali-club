# Content Roadmap - Other Bali - 2026-08-25

## Operating Principle

Prioritize pages that improve real traveller decisions and can be kept accurate. Do not scale pages only for long-tail coverage or AI fan-out variants.

## Content QA Checklist

- Direct answer near the top.
- Clear entities: area, venue type, traveller scenario, date-sensitive facts.
- Evidence or method included where claims are comparative.
- Trade-offs and limitations next to recommendations.
- Internal links to the next action.
- Structured data matches visible content.
- No invented reviews, ratings, hours, prices, or addresses.
