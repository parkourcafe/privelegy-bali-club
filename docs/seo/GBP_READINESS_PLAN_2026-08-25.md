## Critical Policy Position

Other Bali should not create or optimize a Google Business Profile unless the real-world entity is eligible under Google's business representation rules. A website-only travel guide without a staffed customer-facing location may not be eligible for a standard local storefront profile. If there is a real staffed office or a legitimate service-area operation, document it before creation.

## Review Operations Guardrails

- Ask only customers with a genuine experience.
