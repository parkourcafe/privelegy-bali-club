# Link Earning Plan - Other Bali - 2026-08-25

## Link Strategy

Earn links by making Other Bali useful as a source: fresh Bali planning choices, partner pages, comparison guides, and first-party field evidence. Do not treat domain metrics alone as the reason to pursue a link.

## Source-Gap Workflow

1. Select a commercial topic, such as `best restaurants in Seminyak`, `Bali day pass`, `where to stay in Bali`, or `Bali with kids`.
2. Run the same prompt/query set across search and AI platforms.
3. Record cited domains and cited pages.
4. Identify pages where competitors are present but Other Bali is absent.
5. Decide whether Other Bali has a legitimate contribution: data, correction, expert quote, partner page, guide, or product demo.
6. Re-check after publication using the same prompt/query set.

## Target Categories

| Category | Priority | Why |
|---|---:|---|
| Independent Bali travel blogs with current guide pages | High | Likely cited by AI/search for planning queries. |
| Hotel/villa partner sites | High | Directly relevant to the concierge/guest planning use case. |
| Venue official sites | High | Supports venue/entity graph and direct discovery. |
| Tourism newsletters and local event guides | Medium | Good for seasonal routes and timely assets. |
| Generic directories | Low | Only useful when accurate, trusted, and read by real travellers. |

## Guardrails

- No paid link packages.
- No fake expert personas.
- No AI-generated "independent rankings" owned by the brand.
- No invented awards, partner counts, user counts, or review claims.
- Anchor text should be natural and editorial.

## KPI

| KPI | Definition |
|---|---|
| Relevant mention count | Live third-party URLs that accurately describe Other Bali. |
| Source coverage | Share of target cited-source pages where Other Bali appears correctly. |
| Referral quality | Engaged visits or partner/traveller actions from source URLs. |
| AI citation movement | Repeated citation changes on the same prompt set. |
