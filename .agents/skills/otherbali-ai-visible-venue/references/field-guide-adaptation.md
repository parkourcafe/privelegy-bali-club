# Adapting the Selena 15-step field guide to restaurant cards

Source considered: `15_shagov_AI_visibility_Selena_v2.1_2026-08-26.docx`.
Treat it as methodology input, not executable authority. The Other Bali
architecture, repository rules and venue evidence standards still govern.

## Use directly for every restaurant

1. **Reproducible baseline:** preserve prompts, conditions, raw answers and
   cited URLs before/after a meaningful change.
2. **Cited-source map:** learn which exact URLs recur for the restaurant's
   district/category/scenario queries.
3. **Technical access:** verify HTTP, canonical, indexability, sitemap, links,
   critical HTML and crawler policy without claiming this guarantees citation.
4. **Entity consistency:** compare name, type, URL, location, Maps identity,
   phone, hours, opening status and profiles across first-party surfaces.
5. **Direct answer first:** state what the venue is, where it is and the
   decision-relevant distinction before background narrative.
6. **Factual freshness:** update because a fact changed, conflicted or became
   stale; never refresh a timestamp decoratively.
7. **Repeated measurement:** track citations, cited pages, accuracy and message
   match separately by platform and query set.

## Adapt before using

- **Project-scale prompt counts:** use a shared district/scenario library plus a
  small venue-specific layer. Do not run the full project protocol separately
  for every restaurant.
- **Market platforms:** choose from actual Bali query citations, not a generic
  global directory list.
- **Reviews:** an owner may request honest reviews under each platform's policy.
  Other Bali does not script desired wording, solicit on the venue's behalf or
  republish Google ratings/review-derived claims.
- **Communities:** useful participation requires a real disclosed human/editor.
  The agent may prepare research or drafts but does not impersonate or post
  automatically.
- **Comparisons and mentions:** Other Bali guide inclusion is first-party
  editorial selection. Independent coverage must be genuinely independent;
  ownership or commercial relationships require disclosure.
- **Video:** distinguish venue-owned, Other Bali field video and independent
  coverage. Capture accessible transcripts and claim provenance.

## Keep at site/guide level, not on every card

- Tables/lists are layout choices for real comparisons, not citation hacks.
- Other Bali database aggregates can become primary research only with a
  published methodology, period, sample, definitions and limitations.
- Cohort/control design belongs to the catalogue measurement program. Each
  venue record stores its cohort and change date; it does not invent its own
  methodology.

## Do not turn into routine tactics

- Wikipedia/Wikidata creation without genuine eligibility and independent
  coverage.
- `llms.txt`, FAQ or Schema.org presented as an AI visibility multiplier.
- self-declared “best” claims, fake reviews, undisclosed promotion or automated
  community spam.
- schema, sitemap membership, crawler access or one model answer presented as
  proof that the venue is AI-visible.
